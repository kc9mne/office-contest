Plan at a glance

Decided

  • No logins. One vote per device, and voters type their name.
  • Voter list for admins, with likely duplicates flagged.
  • Overall = total of all category votes. One entry can win several categories, and ties share a place.
  • Photo approval is a setting, off by default.
  • Three modes: Halloween, Holiday party, General.
  • Videos: stored on our servers (up to 300 MB) or YouTube links.
  • Branding: logo, company name, optional brand color.
  • Admin access: secret link plus PIN.
  • Photobooth page for a tablet or webcam PC, with AI restyling.

Photobooth flow

  • Booth page on a tablet or PC takes the photo in the browser
  • Our server stores it and sends it to the AI image service with the chosen look's instructions
  • The API key stays on the server and never reaches the booth or phones
  • The restyled photo comes back, is saved, and appears in the gallery

How each mode differs

  • Colors and wording (costume, sweater, dish)
  • Starter categories, editable by admins
  • Event name (parade, sweater walk, tasting)
  • Photobooth looks: spooky or festive; booth off by default for General

Data to store

  • Site: company name, logo, brand color, admin PIN
  • Contest: mode, title, dates, categories, settings, status
  • Entry: name, dept, entry name, photo, approved
  • Vote: voter name, device ID, network, category, entry, time
  • Video: file or YouTube URL, caption, posted by
  • Booth photo: original, AI version, look, name

Still to decide

  • Can people vote for their own entry?
  • Who can post videos?
  • Personal or company AI account, and a daily photo limit
  • Is sending employee photos to an AI service OK under company policy?
